**Environment issues, not code bugs**. They limit what can be tested until they're resolved:

1. **No Ollama anywhere.** The gateway container is configured for
   `OLLAMA=container` (`http://ollama:11434`), but no Ollama container is
   running, and nothing listens on host `:11434`. Every `local` class call,
   T2, local embeddings and the summary narrative (`SUMMARY_MODEL=local`) fail.
   This explains 9 of the 11 contract-suite failures.
2. **The gateway's Gemini key is invalid** (Google: `API_KEY_INVALID`). The
   `cloud`/`auto` chains fail over to Anthropic. It explains the other 2
   contract failures (`gemini-nullable-schema`, `gap-gemini-thinking-cap`).
3. **No ONNX artifact for the classifier's T1** (`./models` is empty, lexicon
   fallback). Since CL-005, nearly every text escalates to T3 (about $0.004 each).
   Exporting the model (`scripts/export_zeroshot_model.py --output ./models`)
   restores a cheap, accurate T1.
4. The orchestrator runs its sessions on the `memory` backend under `--reload`.
   Since OR-004, a restart 404s live sessions; the console now handles that.
5. **Done at start of SIT:** the classifier had no gateway token
   (`MODEL_GATEWAY__TOKEN` was commented out in its `.env`), so T2/T3 were
   never built. Set to `air-classifier-dev`.

The **High** findings, all fixed and verified:

- **OR-001**: card numbers weren't masked on input. They were stored and forwarded to the classifier, the gateway and Anthropic.
- **OR-002**: the orchestrator's turn `max_cost_usd` was ignored (31× overspend).
- **CL-001**: the classifier's `max_cost_usd` was checked only after spending (4× overspend).

**Regression caught during re-test:** CL-006i plus CL-005 meant no verdict
was cached while T2 was down, so repeats paid T3. The classifier session fixed it
with `settled`, and it's verified.

**air-web-console:** encoded dot-segments (`..%2F..%2F`) in proxy paths were
forwarded upstream verbatim. They could escape a path-prefixed base URL into
sibling services. They're now refused (`src/lib/http/upstreamPath.ts`, 10 tests). The
Orchestrator tab also drops a dead session id on the new 404, and the Model Gateway
tab gains `max-tokens-huge` and `empty-content` rejection scenarios
(MG-013).
